## 1. Chart of accounts (kontni plan / računski plan)

**Legal status.** The chart of accounts is *not* prescribed by law for ordinary companies. The current Zakon o računovodstvu (ZOR, NN 85/24, amended NN 145/24) only requires a dnevnik, glavna knjiga and pomoćne knjige (čl. 11–12) and contains no chart-of-accounts provision (the word "kontni plan" appears only for central counterparties). The 2015 ZOR (NN 78/15) čl. 11 had introduced a mandatory "jedinstveni okvirni kontni plan" (OSFI even published one in 2016), but čl. 11 and its penalty were deleted by NN 120/16 before it ever applied. RRiF (RRiF-plus d.o.o.), TEB and HZRIF/RiF each publish their own plans; they are publisher conventions built on the Pravilnik's Bilanca/RDG structure. The accountant's numbering matches **RRiF-ov računski plan za poduzetnike, XXVIII. izdanje (2025)** (Brkanić/Cirkveni Filipović). The free PDF has three-digit synthetic accounts plus suggested analytics; the Bilanca/RDG position column exists only in the paid edition, so the account→AOP mapping in §2 is derived from position names, not copied from RRiF.

## 2. Annual financial statements (GFI-POD) for 2025

**Acts.** ZOR NN 85/24 + 145/24 (FINA's 2025 upute cite exactly these two). New **Pravilnik o strukturi i sadržaju godišnjih financijskih izvještaja, NN 107/2025** (issued under ZOR čl. 18 st. 13) — čl. 10: applies to reporting periods beginning **1 January 2025** or later; čl. 11 repeals NN 95/16, 144/20, 158/23. HSFI: Odluka o objavljivanju HSFI NN 86/15, 105/20, 9/21 (ispravak), 150/22, 84/24 (HSFI 18 from 1.1.2025). ZOR čl. 16: micro/small/medium apply HSFI.

**Size.** ZOR čl. 5 st. 3: micro = does not exceed two of three: ukupna aktiva 450.000,00 EUR, netoprihod 900.000,00 EUR, 10 radnika (measured on the last day of the *preceding* year, čl. 5 st. 1). Čl. 5 st. 9: the three indicators for the current and prior year must be disclosed in the bilješke.

**Which statements.** ZOR čl. 18 st. 3: "mali i mikro poduzetnik dužan je sastaviti bilancu, račun dobiti i gubitka i bilješke" — **cash-flow (NT) and changes in equity (PK) are not required**; HSFI users never prepare OSD (čl. 18 st. 4). Pravilnik čl. 3 st. 1–2 repeats this (NT and PK only for medium/large/consolidating). ZOR čl. 21 st. 2: micro/small need no godišnji izvještaj/izvještaj poslovodstva, but must state in the bilješke information on the purchase of own shares/udjeli.

**Deadlines and channel (ZOR).** Čl. 50 st. 1: bilanca, RDG and **dodatni podaci** to FINA for statistical purposes by **30 April** (svrha predaje 1). Čl. 47 st. 1–5: a trgovačko društvo must also file for javna objava within **six months** of year-end (svrha 2), attaching (st. 4) the **odluka o utvrđivanju GFI** and the **prijedlog odluke o upotrebi dobiti ili pokriću gubitka**. Čl. 50 st. 2: one combined filing by 30 April (svrha 3) satisfies both. Čl. 47 st. 9: inactive company → Izjava o neaktivnosti by 30 April. Čl. 48 st. 6: statements are signed with a qualified e-signature by all directors. Penalties čl. 60: 1.320,00–13.270,00 EUR for the company, 660,00–2.650,00 EUR for the responsible person. FINA's javna objava fee was abolished (ZOR amendment NN 42/20). Filing is through the RGFI web application via e-Građani (the old RGFI app no longer accepts 2025 filings — FINA notice).

**FINA form.** GFI-POD **version 6.0.2** (Excel, released 6 March 2026, "od 2025. godine"; new NKD 2025 code field and auditor-opinion field; cannot be used for earlier years). Sheets: RefStr (reference page), Bilanca (POD-BIL), RDG (POD-RDG), Dodatni podaci (POD-DOP), NT, PK, plus OSD rows. FINA upute §5.2: a micro d.o.o. fills Bilanca, RDG and Dodatni podaci for statistics; for public disclosure Bilanca and RDG plus the "nestandardna dokumentacija" (Bilješke, Odluka o utvrđivanju GFI, Odluka o prijedlogu raspodjele dobiti ili pokriću gubitka) as PDF — Dodatni podaci are not part of the public filing. Reference-page codes: oznaka veličine 1 = mikro; vrsta izvještaja 10; šifra svrhe 1/2/3; standard HSFI; šifra NKD-a per DZS (NKD 2025); prosječan broj zaposlenih = average of headcount on 1.1., 31.3., 30.6., 30.9., 31.12. (÷5).

## 3. Corporate income tax (porez na dobit) for 2025

Zakon o porezu na dobit (ZPD): NN 177/04, 90/05, 57/06, 146/08, 80/10, 22/12, 148/13, 143/14, 50/16, 115/16, 106/18, 121/19, 32/20, 138/20, 114/22, 114/23, 151/25 (the last effective 1.1.2026, i.e. after FY2025). Pravilnik o porezu na dobit (PPD): NN 95/05 … 156/23, 16/25 (in force 1.2.2025).

- **Rate** — ZPD čl. 28: **10 %** if revenues (prihodi) in the tax period are up to 1.000.000,00 EUR; **18 %** if equal to or above 1.000.000,00 EUR. The test is total revenue (RDG AOP 180), not profit.
- **Increases of the tax base** — ZPD čl. 7 st. 1: t. 2 amortizacija above čl. 12; t. 3 **50 % of reprezentacija** (PPD čl. 23 defines it: gifts, hospitality, leisure for business partners); t. 4 **50 % of costs of own or leased motor vehicles for personal transport** (osobni automobili) except insurance, interest, road taxes and the input VAT already non-deductible (PPD čl. 25); t. 5 shortages above HGK norms; t. 6 troškovi prisilne naplate poreza; t. 7 kazne; t. 8 **zatezne kamate između povezanih osoba** (ordinary late-payment interest to the state on account 4741 is caught by t. 6/t. 13 "rashodi koji nisu u svezi s ostvarivanjem dobiti" — practitioners treat 4741 as non-deductible; **UNVERIFIED** which točka the accountant used); t. 10 **darovanja above 2 % of prior-year revenue** (PPD čl. 30; account 4860 is within the limit by name); t. 11 non-deductible interest under čl. 8 (thin-cap 4× member's capital share, ≥25 % holders) and čl. 14; t. 12 **skrivene isplate dobiti** (PPD čl. 26.a: keep a register of private-purpose costs with date, name, OIB, type, amount); t. 13 any other expense not related to earning profit.
- **Loss carry-forward** — čl. 17: negative base = porezni gubitak, carried forward and offset in the following **five years**, oldest first.
- **Advances** — čl. 34 st. 1: "Predujam se plaća mjesečno do kraja mjeseca za protekli mjesec, u svoti koja se dobije kada se porezna obveza za prethodno porezno razdoblje podijeli s brojem mjeseci istoga razdoblja." So `predujam_2026 = PD2025 row "porezna obveza" / months of operation in 2025` (12 for a full year), applied from the month after filing (first new instalment for April, paid by 31 May); st. 2 Porezna may change it by rješenje on request; st. 3 a new taxpayer pays none until the first PD.
- **Filing** — čl. 35: PD filed within **four months** after the period (= **30 April 2026** for FY2025), the difference paid by the same date; overpaid advances refunded or credited. Filing only via **ePorezna** (mandatory electronic filing since 1.1.2025, Porezna uputa). Attachments: PD-IPO if any related-party transactions; GFI are not attached to PD (they go to FINA) but PD lines 1–2 must reconcile to RDG AOP 180/181.
- **Obrazac PD rows** (Porezna/RRiF uputa; form unchanged for 2025): 1 Ukupni prihodi · 2 Ukupni rashodi · 3 Dobit · 4 Gubitak · II. Povećanja: 5 amortizacija iznad čl. 12 · 6 50 % troškova reprezentacije · 7 50 % nepriznatih troškova za osobni prijevoz · 8 manjkovi · 9 rashodi utvrđeni u nadzoru · 10 troškovi prisilne naplate · 11 kazne · 12 zatezne kamate između povezanih osoba · 13 povlastice i imovinske koristi · 14 darovanja iznad dopuštenih · 15 kamate na zajmove dioničara/članova (čl. 8) · 16–27 further increases (kamate čl. 14, skrivene isplate, ostalo) · III. Umanjenja (dividend income, reinvested profit, state aid) · IV. Porezna osnovica (Porezna's 2024 uputa cites r.br. 36) · preneseni gubitak · osnovica nakon gubitka · V. stopa, porezna obveza, olakšice, uplaćeni predujmovi (r.br. 56 in the 2024 uputa), razlika za uplatu/povrat, predujam za sljedeće razdoblje. **UNVERIFIED**: exact numbering of rows 16 onward — read them from the ePorezna PD form.
- **PD-IPO** — PPD čl. 47.b: a taxpayer that had business events with povezane osobe (ZPD čl. 13 st. 2: participation in management, control or capital — includes a natural-person member) files Obrazac PD-IPO with the PD (by 30 April). Section I.1 obveze po primljenim zajmovima (columns incl. 4× capital test, kamatna stopa, kamate iznad čl. 14), I.2 potraživanja po danim zajmovima (columns: stanje glavnice 1.1./31.12., kamatna stopa, kamate tijekom razdoblja, povećanje osnovice za kamate iz čl. 14), II dobra i usluge. Loans at the same rate to the same party may be aggregated. No PD-IPO if there were no related-party transactions.

## 4. Loans to and private spending by the member

- **Prescribed related-party interest** — ZPD čl. 14 st. 1–3: interest income on loans to related parties at least, and interest expense at most, the rate the minister publishes before the period. PPD čl. 37: the rate = arithmetic mean of the ECB main-refinancing rate in the current year. **2025: 4,38 %** (Odluka NN 151/24, corrected by Ispravak NN 154/24 which fixed "2024"→"2025"). **2026: 2,65 %** (Odluka NN 150/25, in force 31.12.2025). ZPD čl. 13 st. 5 limits čl. 13–14 between two Croatian residents to cases where one has a preferential tax status or a loss carry-forward; the rule is aimed at loans to/from *entities*.
- **Loan to the member who is a natural person** — Zakon o porezu na dohodak (ZPDoh) čl. 21 st. 3: "Primitkom po osnovi povoljnijih kamata smatra se razlika između ugovorene niže i stope kamate od **2 % godišnje**" — for the director-employee, interest below 2 % is plaća u naravi (taxed as salary with contributions). Practitioners (mojafirma.hr, TEB) state that the 2 % floor, not the 4,38 % MoF rate, governs owner loans; for a non-employee member the shortfall is treated as dohodak od kapitala. **UNVERIFIED**: the exact ZPDoh stavak for the non-employee case. Interest earned is 7710/1243; the loan sits in 11506 (member of uprava/employee) or 115091; the loan needs a written contract, repayment terms and reporting in PD-IPO.
- **Private expenses paid by the company** — two treatments: (a) book as a receivable from the member (1331 "Potraživanja od članova društva za privatne troškove") to be repaid/settled against dividends, bearing at least 2 % interest — no tax if genuinely repaid; or (b) treat as **skrivena isplata dobiti / izuzimanje**: non-deductible (ZPD čl. 7 st. 1 t. 12, PPD čl. 26.a register) and taxed on the member as dohodak od kapitala at **36 %** (ZPDoh čl. 66 st. 1 "izuzimanja imovine i korištenja usluga na teret dobiti"; čl. 70 rates), withheld and reported on JOPPD. RRiF account 24310 is exactly this 36 % liability. A running balance on 1331 that is never repaid is what a nadzor reclassifies to (b).
- **Dividends / udjeli u dobiti** — ZPDoh čl. 66–70: capital income, **12 %** withholding at payment, final tax (no annual assessment, no contributions; prirez abolished from 2024). Payer books 2010 → 24311 (12 %) and pays tax at payment; **JOPPD on the day of payment or the next working day** (Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak; TEB/REGOS example: oznaka stjecatelja 6.1 = 1001, oznaka primitka 6.2 = 1001 — **UNVERIFIED** against the current JOPPD code lists). Exempt: profits realised up to 29.2.2012 (except 2001–2004) (Porezna uprava). ZPD čl. 31: no porez po odbitku on dividends to resident natural persons (that is the 12 % income tax); 10 % applies to foreign legal persons. Requires a skupština decision (ZTD čl. 441; sole member: written decision in the knjiga odluka, ZTD čl. 445).

## 5. Depreciation and small-value assets

ZPD čl. 12: st. 3 dugotrajna imovina = cost **above 665,00 EUR** and useful life over one year; st. 5 annual rates: građevinski objekti i brodovi >1000 BRT 5 %; osnovno stado, osobni automobili 20 %; nematerijalna imovina, oprema, vozila (osim automobila), mehanizacija **25 %**; **računala, računalna oprema i programi, mobilni telefoni i oprema za računalne mreže 50 %**; ostala nespomenuta imovina 10 %; st. 6 rates **may be doubled** (so 100 % for computers/phones/software, 50 % for office equipment) and remain deductible; lower book depreciation is also deductible; personal cars: depreciation deductible only up to **54.000,00 EUR** cost per vehicle (čl. 12). Assets ≤ 665,00 EUR may be expensed at once (sitni inventar: 3500 on receipt, 3600 in use, written off through 363/3630 against 4040) — so office phones under 665 EUR are expensed, above it they are 0311/0312 at 50 % (100 % doubled). Assets in preparation (0371) are not depreciated until available for use (HSFI 6 — **UNVERIFIED** point number).

## 9. What is different for 2026

- **Zakon o fiskalizaciji NN 89/25** (in force 1.9.2025; eRačun provisions from 1.1.2026): a VAT-registered taxpayer must **issue and receive eRačuni for all domestic B2B/B2G supplies** (čl. 36–41) through an informacijski posrednik (čl. 58–59), each line carrying a **KPD 2025** classification code; the eRačun is **fiscalised** to Porezna at issue and by the recipient within 5 working days (čl. 48); **payment data reported by the 20th of the following month** (čl. 53) and rejections likewise (čl. 52). Cross-border invoices to EU/third-country customers are **not** eRačuni and are not fiscalised (čl. 3 st. 3 "tuzemne transakcije"; Porezna FAQ) — **UNVERIFIED** whether any eIzvještavanje row must be sent for them. Non-VAT taxpayers receive from 2026 and issue from 2027 (free MIKROeRAČUN, čl. 55–56). Penalties čl. 71–73: up to 66.360 EUR. Consequence for the software: every domestic incoming invoice from 2026 should exist as a received eRačun and the 2026 books must carry payment-status reporting.
- **PDV (NN 151/25, 1.1.2026)**: filing deadline moved to the last day of the following month; U-RA and PPO abolished; no more consent needed for e-invoices (čl. 80).
- **ZPD (NN 151/25, 1.1.2026)**: amendments to čl. 2, 6, 7, 13, 14.a (advance pricing agreements), 30, 32, 33, 35 — check the FY2026 PD; rates and thresholds unchanged. **OPZ** amended by NN 151/25 (čl. 8, 64, 67, 79, 124, 191–194).
- **NKD 2025** (Odluka NN 47/24; Pravilnik o razvrstavanju NN 81/24) applies from 1.1.2025 — the 2025 GFI-POD and Dodatni podaci already use the new 5-digit codes; the DZS reassigned every subject's code.
- Contribution bases 2026 (NN 150/25, §6); related-party rate 2,65 % (§4); Viškovo 20/30 unchanged; income-tax personal-allowance changes announced for 2026 concern dependants (**UNVERIFIED** detail).
- Accounting: HSFI 18 (going concern) applies from 1.1.2025 (NN 84/24); Pravilnik NN 107/2025 governs both FY2025 and FY2026 statements.

## 10. Bilješke and the two decisions

- **Bilješke** — legal basis: ZOR čl. 18 st. 3; ZOR čl. 5 st. 9 (size indicators for current and prior year); ZOR čl. 21 st. 2 (own shares/udjeli); Pravilnik NN 107/2025 čl. 5 (notes follow the order of Bilanca/RDG items, disclose relations when an item spans positions); Pravilnik čl. 6–7 apply only to large/PIE. The binding minimum for micro/small is **HSFI 1 t. 1.42** (NN 105/20, from 2021): (1) naziv, sjedište, pravni oblik, država osnivanja, MBS, OIB, likvidacija status; (2) opis djelatnosti; (3) osnova sastavljanja (HSFI, EUR); (4) usvojene računovodstvene politike (incl. depreciation methods/rates); (5) revalorizacija movements; (6) fair-value info; (7) total off-balance financial obligations, jamstva, nepredviđeni izdaci; (8) **predujmovi i krediti članovima uprave** with rates and terms (the member loan belongs here); (9) exceptional income/expense items; (10) debts due after more than five years and secured debts; (11) prosječan broj radnika; (12) related-party transactions. OSFI publishes a **standardized bilješke template** for micro/small entities (adopted 6.6.2023, on osfi.hr; a recommendation, not mandatory; FINA links it from the forms page). FINA accepts the notes as a PDF attached to the public filing.
- **Odluka o utvrđivanju godišnjih financijskih izvještaja** — required by ZOR čl. 47 st. 4, stored in the Registar (čl. 51 st. 4 t. 6). Adopted by the skupština (ZTD čl. 441 st. 1) — for a single-member d.o.o. the sole member adopts it in writing and enters it in the knjiga odluka (ZTD čl. 445). Content (RRiF template): legal basis (ZTD čl. 441, ZOR čl. 47 st. 4), that the bilanca, račun dobiti i gubitka and bilješke for the year ending 31.12.2025 are adopted, stating ukupna aktiva/pasiva and dobit/gubitak nakon oporezivanja (= AOP 065/126 and 187/188), date, place, signature of the member/chair.
- **Odluka o upotrebi dobiti (ili pokriću gubitka)** — ZOR čl. 47 st. 4 (filed as a *prijedlog* if not yet adopted), ZTD čl. 441. Content: the dobit poslovne godine amount, its allocation to zadržana dobit (940/94010), dividends to members (2010 → 12 % tax, §4) and/or reserves; ZOR čl. 18 st. 12 requires first covering unamortised development costs from profit. A d.o.o. has no statutory legal-reserve percentage (the 25 % rule of ZTD čl. 390.a is for j.d.o.o.; d.d. rules are in čl. 222) — **UNVERIFIED** that the društveni ugovor imposes none. For a loss: amount and source of coverage (zadržana dobit/rezerve) or carried forward as preneseni gubitak (941). Both decisions are uploaded as PDFs with the public filing; the same figures must match the GFI-POD.
