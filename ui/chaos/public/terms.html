What this is

An internal application built and operated by Inorbit d.o.o. for its own bookkeeping: it consolidates the company's own bank transactions, collects the supplier invoices addressed to it, and produces the invoices it issues to its clients.

Who may use it

Only authorised personnel of Inorbit d.o.o. It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no user accounts are offered to anyone outside the company, and no service is provided to third parties. Access requires authentication, and unauthorised access is not permitted.

Bank account access

Where the application reads bank account information, it does so under PSD2 through a licensed Account Information Service Provider, and only for accounts the account holder has explicitly authorised through their own bank. Access is read-only: the application does not initiate payments or move money. Consent is time-limited, must be renewed periodically, and may be withdrawn at any time, which stops all further access.

Accuracy and responsibility

The application assists with bookkeeping; it does not replace professional accounting or tax advice. Figures it produces are derived from data supplied by banks and suppliers and may be incomplete, delayed or incorrect.
