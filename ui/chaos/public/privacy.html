What this application is

An internal bookkeeping tool built and operated by Inorbit d.o.o. for its own accounting. It is used by a single person — the company's director — and is not offered, sold or made available to any third party. There is no public sign-up.

What data it processes

  • Bank account information for accounts belonging to Inorbit d.o.o. and to its director personally: account identifiers (IBAN), account names, balances, and transaction records including dates, amounts, currencies, counterparty names and payment references.
  • Supplier invoices and receipts addressed to Inorbit d.o.o., collected from the company's own mailbox and from suppliers' billing portals.
  • Invoices issued by Inorbit d.o.o., including client business details.

Transaction records inevitably contain the names of counterparties. Where a counterparty is a natural person, that name is personal data and is treated as such.

How bank data is obtained

Account information is retrieved under PSD2 through a licensed Account Information Service Provider. Access is granted only by the account holder, through the bank's own authentication, and is limited to the specific accounts they authorise. That consent is time-limited and must be renewed periodically. It can be withdrawn at any time, either in this application or directly with the bank; withdrawal stops all further access.

Why it is processed

  • Keeping the company's books and preparing records for its accountant.
  • Categorising expenses and matching supplier invoices to the payments that settled them.
  • Meeting statutory accounting, tax and invoicing obligations under Croatian law.

The legal bases are the account holder's consent for accessing bank account information, legal obligation for retaining accounting records, and the company's legitimate interest in managing its own finances.

What is not done with it

  • It is not sold, rented or shared for anyone else's commercial purposes.
  • It is not used for advertising, profiling or automated decisions with legal effects.
  • It is not transferred to any party other than those listed below.

Who else is involved

  • The licensed Account Information Service Provider that carries the connection to the bank.
  • The company's external accountant, who receives the accounting records they need.
  • The infrastructure provider hosting the application.

Data is stored within the European Economic Area.

How long it is kept

Accounting records — invoices, supporting documents and the transactions they relate to — are retained for as long as Croatian accounting and tax legislation requires. Data that is not part of an accounting record is deleted when it is no longer needed for the purposes above.

Security

Access requires authentication and is limited to the company's director. Credentials used to reach the bank are held as secrets, are never written to application logs, and are not included in any data export. Traffic is encrypted in transit.

Your rights

Anyone whose personal data appears here may request access to it, correction of it, erasure of it, restriction of its processing, or a copy in a portable form, and may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Where processing rests on consent, that consent can be withdrawn at any time without affecting what was lawful beforehand.

Requests go to nevio@inorbit.hr. There is also a right to complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is published at this address with a new date.
